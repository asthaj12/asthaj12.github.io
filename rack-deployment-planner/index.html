Scenario: What If a Task Slips 4 Weeks?

Choose a task. Its duration rises by 4 weeks, both passes are recomputed, and the project finish, critical path and rack ramp update together. The commit date does not move.

Methodology: Formulas and Limits

Forward pass ES_i = max over predecessors p of EF_p (0 if no predecessor) EF_i = ES_i + d_i Project finish T = max_i EF_i Backward pass LF_i = min over successors s of LS_s (T if no successor) LS_i = LF_i − d_i Total float TF_i = LS_i − ES_i = LF_i − EF_i Critical TF_i = 0. Critical path = every chain of critical tasks where EF_p = ES_s, from a start to a finish at T.
Slip scenario d_k → d_k + 4 for the selected task k, then rerun both passes. ΔT = T_scenario − T_base Capacity racks live at end of week w = min(48, 16 × (w − ⌈T⌉ + 1)) commit week = ⌈T_base⌉ + 1 (fixed; a slip does not move it) on track = racks live at commit week; at risk = 48 − on track
  • Durations are deterministic and the network is a single finish-to-start chain. Lags, resource leveling and calendar gaps are not modeled.
  • One task slips at a time. Float is total float, not free float, so a slip on a near-critical task can move the finish by only the part of its float it exceeds.
  • Gate and risk tables are status records. They are not linked to the computed dates.
  • All tasks, durations and owners are synthetic.
